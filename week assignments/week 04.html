ProtoSem Journal - Week 04

Laser Cutting, 3D Printing and Alpha Team Formation

This week focused on exploring digital fabrication techniques through laser cutting and 3D printing, while also marking an important milestone with the final formation of the Alpha team. The practical sessions helped me understand how digital designs can be converted into physical components and how teamwork plays an important role in engineering projects.

What I learned

  • Understood the basic working principles and applications of laser cutting and 3D printing.
  • Learned how digital designs can be prepared and converted into suitable formats for fabrication.
  • Gained practical experience with laser cutting, including design preparation, machine settings, and fabrication.
  • Explored the basic workflow of 3D printing, from digital model preparation to physical output.
  • Understood the importance of accuracy, material selection, machine parameters, and fabrication tolerances.
  • Completed the final Alpha team formation, establishing a team structure for upcoming collaborative projects.
  • Improved my understanding of how design, fabrication, and teamwork work together in practical engineering.

Big takeaway

Digital fabrication provides a direct connection between CAD design and real-world manufacturing. Laser cutting and 3D printing showed me how a digital model can be transformed into a physical component while considering factors such as dimensions, material properties, machine parameters, and manufacturing limitations. The Alpha team formation also highlighted the importance of communication, responsibility, and collaboration in completing engineering projects successfully.

Learning reflection

Laser cutting

During the laser cutting session, I learned how a digital design is prepared for fabrication and converted into a physical part using a laser cutting machine. I gained a better understanding of how factors such as power, speed, focus, kerf, material thickness, and design dimensions can affect the final output. This activity helped me recognize the importance of preparing accurate digital files before starting the manufacturing process.

3D printing

The 3D printing activity introduced me to another important digital manufacturing method. I learned how a 3D CAD model can be converted into printable layers and produced as a physical object. The process helped me understand the importance of model accuracy, print orientation, layer settings, supports, and material considerations when producing a functional part.

Alpha team formation

Another important part of Week 4 was the final formation of the Alpha team. Working as part of a team requires effective communication, coordination, responsibility, and the ability to contribute individual skills toward a common objective. This team formation provided a foundation for upcoming projects where collaboration and problem-solving will be essential.

Final reflection

Week 4 was a valuable combination of digital fabrication, practical manufacturing, and teamwork. Laser cutting and 3D printing helped me understand how CAD designs can be transformed into real-world components, while the Alpha team formation strengthened my understanding of collaborative engineering. Overall, this week improved both my technical confidence and my readiness to work on more advanced practical projects.
