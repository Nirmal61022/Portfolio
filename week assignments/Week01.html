Week 01

5S Activity and Portfolio Development

During the first week of the internship, we participated in a 5S workplace organization activity for the first three days. I was assigned to the DFAB Team, where our responsibility was to sort and organize different types of screws, nails, chuck bits, mechanical tools, and power tools. We identified the various items, grouped them according to their type and purpose, and arranged them systematically.

On the second day, after completing the major sorting activities, we continued organizing the tools and hardware. We named and labeled the different boxes and storage sections so that each item could be easily identified and accessed. This activity helped us understand the importance of proper organization and teamwork in maintaining an efficient workspace.

On the third day, we arranged all the organized screws, nails, chuck bits, mechanical tools, and power tools neatly in the storage area. Each box and section was properly labeled, making the items easy to locate and use. This activity demonstrated the practical application of the 5S principles and resulted in a cleaner, safer, and more organized work environment.

During the remaining two days of the week, we began developing our personal portfolios. We worked on presenting our educational background, technical skills, projects, achievements, and career objectives in a professional format. This activity helped us improve our self-presentation skills and prepare for future career opportunities.

Overall, the first week enhanced my understanding of workplace organization, teamwork, and professional profile development. The 5S activity also taught me the importance of proper sorting, labeling, and arrangement of tools and materials for maintaining an efficient and organized work environment.
