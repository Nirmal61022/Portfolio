ProtoSem Journal - Week 01

5S Activity and Portfolio Development

During the first week of the internship, we participated in a 5S workplace organization activity for the first three days. I was assigned to the DFAB Team, where we sorted and organized screws, nails, chuck bits, mechanical tools, and power tools according to their type and purpose.

What I learned

  • Applied the 5S principles through sorting, labeling, and systematic arrangement.
  • Improved my understanding of teamwork and an efficient workplace.
  • Learned to keep tools and materials easy to identify, access, and use.
  • Started developing a professional portfolio and improving self-presentation skills.

Big takeaway

Proper organization creates a cleaner, safer, and more efficient workspace. Good teamwork and clear systems make every task easier to complete.

Week 1 in pictures

Learning reflection

5S workplace organization

After completing the major sorting activities, we named and labeled the boxes and storage sections so that each item could be easily identified and accessed. On the third day, we arranged the organized tools neatly in the storage area. This demonstrated the practical value of 5S principles in maintaining an efficient and safe work environment.

Portfolio development

During the remaining days, we began developing personal portfolios. We worked on presenting our educational background, technical skills, projects, achievements, and career objectives in a professional format. This helped improve our self-presentation skills and prepare for future career opportunities.

Final reflection: Week 1 strengthened my understanding of workplace organization, teamwork, and professional profile development. It taught me that careful sorting, labeling, and arrangement are essential habits for productive engineering work.
