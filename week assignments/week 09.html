ProtoSem Journal – Week 09

Smart India Hackathon (SIH)

This week was dedicated to the Smart India Hackathon (SIH), focusing on project evaluation, technical development, and collaborative problem-solving. We participated in the first and second project reviews, followed by an intensive 36-hour hackathon. These activities helped us evaluate our progress, refine our approach, and work collaboratively toward developing a practical solution to a real-world problem.

What I learned

  • Understood the importance of project reviews in evaluating technical progress and identifying areas for improvement.
  • Improved my understanding of presenting a problem statement and explaining a proposed technical solution.
  • Gained experience in teamwork, task coordination, and collaborative decision-making.
  • Learned the importance of time management and prioritizing tasks during a 36-hour hackathon.
  • Developed a better understanding of applying engineering knowledge to practical challenges.

Big takeaway

Successful innovation requires clear objectives, effective teamwork, continuous evaluation, and the ability to solve problems within limited time and resources.

Learning reflection

Project reviews

The first and second reviews provided opportunities to evaluate our project progress, communicate our ideas, and understand the importance of structured technical feedback. I learned that regular reviews help identify gaps and improve the direction of a project.

36-hour hackathon

Participating in the 36-hour hackathon exposed me to an intensive problem-solving environment. It highlighted the importance of planning, dividing responsibilities, coordinating with teammates, and making effective technical decisions under time constraints.

Teamwork and innovation

The experience reinforced the importance of collaboration in engineering projects. Working toward a common objective requires communication, adaptability, and a shared understanding of the problem and proposed solution.

Final reflection: Week 9 strengthened my understanding of project evaluation, teamwork, and time-bound engineering development. The two project reviews and the 36-hour hackathon provided valuable exposure to collaborative innovation and practical problem-solving, helping me develop skills essential for future engineering projects.
