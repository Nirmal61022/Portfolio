ProtoSem Journal - Week 06

Figma, UI/UX Development, Embedded Systems and Soldering

This week focused on developing skills in UI/UX design using Figma, understanding the fundamentals of computational hardware and embedded systems, and gaining practical experience in soldering. The sessions helped me connect digital interface design with hardware-based engineering and practical electronic assembly.

What I learned

  • Learned the fundamentals of UI/UX design and how user requirements influence interface development.
  • Gained practical experience using Figma to create wireframes, layouts, and user interface designs.
  • Understood the importance of usability, consistency, navigation, and visual hierarchy in UI/UX development.
  • Explored the basic concepts of computational hardware and embedded systems and their role in modern engineering applications.
  • Learned how microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, and other hardware components work together in embedded systems.
  • Gained practical knowledge of soldering techniques, component placement, solder joints, and safe handling of electronic components.
  • Connected software interface design, computational hardware, and electronics through practical engineering activities.

Big takeaway

Good engineering is not limited to hardware or software alone. Effective solutions combine user-centered design, computational thinking, embedded hardware, and practical electronic skills to create functional and usable systems.

Learning reflection

Figma and UI/UX Development

During the Figma session, I learned how digital products can be planned and designed with the user in mind. I explored concepts such as wireframing, layout, navigation, visual hierarchy, and usability. Working with Figma helped me understand how an initial idea can be transformed into a structured and interactive interface before actual development begins.

Computational Hardware and Embedded Systems

The computational hardware and embedded systems session helped me understand how hardware and software interact in engineering applications. I learned about the role of microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, input/output devices, and processing units in embedded systems. This gave me a better understanding of how dedicated computing systems can be designed to monitor inputs and control real-world processes.

Soldering

The soldering practical session gave me hands-on experience with electronic assembly. I learned how to position components correctly, apply solder to create strong electrical and mechanical connections, and inspect solder joints for quality. This activity improved my confidence in handling electronic components and showed me the importance of precision and safety when working with circuits.

Final reflection: Week 6 helped me understand the relationship between design, software, hardware, and electronics. Figma and UI/UX development improved my ability to think from the user's perspective, while embedded systems introduced me to computational hardware and soldering provided practical electronics experience. Together, these activities strengthened my overall understanding of how complete engineering systems are designed, developed, and assembled.
