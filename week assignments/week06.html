ProtoSem Journal - Week 06

Figma, UI/UX Development, Embedded Systems and Soldering

This week connected digital interface design with hardware-based engineering and practical electronic assembly. We developed UI/UX skills using Figma, explored computational hardware and embedded systems, and gained hands-on experience with soldering.

What I learned

  • Learned how user requirements influence UI/UX design and interface development.
  • Gained practical experience using Figma to create wireframes, layouts, and user interfaces.
  • Understood usability, consistency, navigation, and visual hierarchy in interface design.
  • Explored computational hardware, embedded systems, microcontrollers, sensors, and actuators.
  • Practiced component placement, solder joints, and safe handling of electronic components.
  • Connected software interface design, computational hardware, and electronics through practical activities.

Big takeaway

Good engineering combines user-centered design, computational thinking, embedded hardware, and practical electronic skills to create systems that are both functional and usable.

Week 6 in pictures

Learning reflection

Figma and UI/UX development

During the Figma session, I learned how digital products can be planned and designed with the user in mind. I explored wireframing, layout, navigation, visual hierarchy, and usability. Working with Figma helped me understand how an initial idea can be transformed into a structured and interactive interface before development begins.

Computational hardware and embedded systems

The computational hardware and embedded systems session helped me understand how hardware and software interact in engineering applications. I learned about microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, input/output devices, and processing units, and how dedicated computing systems monitor inputs and control real-world processes.

Soldering

The soldering practical session gave me hands-on experience with electronic assembly. I learned how to position components correctly, apply solder to create strong electrical and mechanical connections, and inspect solder joints for quality. This activity improved my confidence in handling electronic components and showed me the importance of precision and safety when working with circuits.

Final reflection: Week 6 helped me understand the relationship between design, software, hardware, and electronics. Figma and UI/UX development improved my ability to think from the user's perspective, while embedded systems introduced me to computational hardware and soldering provided practical electronics experience. Together, these activities strengthened my understanding of how complete engineering systems are designed, developed, and assembled.
